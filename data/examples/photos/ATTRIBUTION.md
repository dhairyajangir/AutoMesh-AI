# Citroën 2CV photo example

Photographs by **Manuel Strehl**, taken 1 October 2005, shared under [Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 2.5 Generic](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/).

- `side.jpg`: [2CV Dolly seite.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2CV_Dolly_seite.jpg), 1785 × 828, original photograph.
- `front.jpg`: [2CV Dolly profil.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2CV_Dolly_profil.jpg), 1353 × 1032, original photograph.

The local filenames have changed. The photographs themselves are unmodified. Generated masks and the derived example geometry are adaptations of these images and are distributed under CC BY-SA 2.5 as well. Credit the photographer, link the license, and indicate subsequent changes when redistributing them. No endorsement by the photographer is implied.

These are perspective photographs, not calibrated orthographic measurements. The front photograph is oblique, with part of the side visible; this is a stress example of capture mismatch, not a recommended capture angle. The example has no top view. Its output is an approximate silhouette model and must not be used as a dimensional reference. For better input, take a straight-on front photograph with both sides equally visible.
